## Risks

- **high** — Joint speed is compared with the vendor no-load speed, which overstates speed at peak torque. Test: Dynamometer test of the chosen actuator at the jump's torque-speed point. _(tied to: no torque-speed curves read from vendors)_
- **high** — Thermal limits: peak torque is a short-duration vendor figure; repeated jumps heat the motors. Test: Repeated-jump thermal soak test with motor temperature logging. _(tied to: Thermal effects not modelled)_
- **high** — The landing GRF limit is an unverified default (no hardware source). Test: Drop test of the leg assembly instrumented with a force plate. _(tied to: landing_grf_limit_bw (unverified default))_
- **med** — Structural strength of links/mounts is a hand calculation only. Test: FEA + proof load of the shank link. _(tied to: Structural strength not analysed (no FEA))_
- **med** — Sensing and actuator latency are not modelled (perfect state feedback). Test: Hardware-in-the-loop test with real encoder/command latency. _(tied to: Perfect state feedback assumed)_
- **med** — Mass of the selected actuators is added on top of the G1 model mass (no sourced per-joint G1 actuator mass to subtract); the battery mass is a placeholder. Test: Weigh the real assembly and re-run the loop. _(tied to: placeholder/unsubtracted masses)_
