## Risks

- **high** — Per-nozzle thrust of the real hardware is unknown; the whole design scales with this swept assumption. Test: Static thrust-stand test of one candidate nozzle + valve at the working pressure. _(tied to: thruster_force_max (no official figure))_
- **high** — Blow-down cooling and pressure droop are not modelled: thrust and Isp fall as the tank empties and the gas cools. Test: Instrumented full-tank blow-down test measuring thrust vs time. _(tied to: Thermal/blow-down effects not modelled; constant-Isp thrust)_
- **med** — Isp (70 s) is an unverified assumption and sets the propellant and tank size. Test: Measure Isp (thrust / mass flow) on the thrust stand. _(tied to: isp (unverified))_
- **high** — The landing contact load comes from a rigid-body contact model without suspension compliance (tens of g); bracket and cradle strength were checked only with hand calculations. Test: FEA of brackets/cradle + drop test of the suspension and mounts at the measured peak contact load. _(tied to: Structural strength not analysed (no FEA))_
- **med** — Aerodynamic pitching moments in flight are not simulated. Test: Wind-tunnel or coast-down test with a scale model / instrumented run. _(tied to: Aerodynamics not simulated)_
- **med** — Real sensing/latency/valve response is not modelled; the controller is open-loop timing windows. Test: Hardware-in-the-loop test of the valve timing and IMU latency. _(tied to: Perfect state feedback assumed)_
- **med** — Placeholder masses (nozzles, valves, regulator) are not sourced; the closed loop uses them. Test: Weigh candidate parts and re-run the loop with measured masses. _(tied to: placeholder masses flagged estimate)_
